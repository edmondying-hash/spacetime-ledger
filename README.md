# The Quantized Space-Ledger
**Locating Quantum Non-Locality in a Discrete Spacetime Substrate**

Edmond Ying · October 2026 · working hypothesis

This paper is a speculative, information-theoretic framework. Its hypothesis is that spacetime is a discrete, entangled network of Planck-scale nodes (a "Space-Ledger") that carries the quantum state. The framework is organized as a client–server picture. The observable world of matter, signals and detectors is the local client side, limited by the speed of light. Spacetime is the server side, which holds the global state, including entanglement and Bell correlations. Non-locality is not removed; it is located in the substrate, while the client side keeps no-signaling.

### Three Bodies on the Ledger Grid: Figure-8 vs Newton — `video/threebody.mp4` (90 s)

*Illustration of a proposed model. Values real; only motion and timing exaggerated.*

Gravity modelled as load that spreads over the 8-link BCC grid, with no pairwise forces: each body moves up the gradient of the other bodies' load. The grid runs are compared with a Newtonian reference.

- **Figure-8, 12 cells per unit:** a stable figure-8 for 4 periods (t = 25.3), ending 0.063 units off Newton (the orbit is ~2 units across). A zoomed inset shows the gap.
- **Pythagorean 3-4-5, 10 cells per unit:** tracks Newton to 0.017 at t = 1.80. At t ≈ 1.9 two bodies pass 0.021 units apart, inside the 0.15-unit load smoothing. This is a grid-resolution limit at a close pass; after it the paths stay apart (1.96 units off at t = 10). The mass-3 body has close passes at t = 2.8, 5.6 and 9.0.
- **Caveat:** the video shows the load spread instantly; in the model it travels at c.

### OP2 star collapse — [video/op2_collapse/op2_collapse_v2.mp4](video/op2_collapse/op2_collapse_v2.mp4) (38 s)

*Illustration of a proposed model. Radial-shell mean-field toy at G = 0.01 (model units).*

Separate added-mass states, bridged on screen by adding mass. Timing and radii are interpolated for the illustration. Supported lump N = 141 (half-mass density ≈ 1.93); denser core N = 3853 (≈ 8.1, about 4×); chosen support cap N ≈ 4738; then a grid-floor core with an extended halo, N ≈ 46,127 (half-mass density ≈ 5,500).

Blast-spill is the load-packing rule for the outer extent. Supernova, ejecta, bounce, black hole, and empirical stellar validation are outside this illustration.
