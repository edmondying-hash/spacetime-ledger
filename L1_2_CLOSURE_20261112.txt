3. WHAT DID NOT PASS AS WRITTEN, STATED PLAINLY
----------------------------------------------------------------
Design R5 (the design's own b ladder with the D3 thresholds) does NOT confirm.
Reasons, both in the design and not in the run:
  (a) The doubler end-mode weight at exactly (+-pi/2)^3 never reaches the 0.5
      "survives" threshold (maximum 0.353 at b = 0.40, N_s = 8; about 0.276 at
      b = 0.40, N_s = 48), so no (b, N_s) is classified "survives". b = 0.30
      and 0.35 therefore classify "allowed".
  (b) b = 0.40 has no reference gap in the design or the pre-registration, so
      it classifies "unresolved (rule input missing)".
The D3 thresholds were proposals made without the data, and Edmond accepted
them as written. They are NOT retuned after the data (plan section 0.1, rule 4).
Design R5 is recorded as failed as written, with the rule mis-specified. L1.2 is
closed on the pre-registration boundary clause, which governs under D4.

What the data do show about the doubler at the point (pi/2, pi/2, +-pi/2),
measured at exactly those points over N_s = 24, 32, 48:
  b = 0.25   gap 0.0243 -> 0.0233, fitted slope -0.0017 per copy
  b = 0.30   gap 0.0131 -> 0.0113, fitted slope -0.0059 per copy (decelerating)
  b = 0.35   gap 0.00418 -> 0.00158, fitted slope -0.040 per copy
  b = 0.40   gap 5.6e-4 -> 1.2e-5, fitted slope -0.16 per copy
The exponential fall is clear from b = 0.35. At b = 0.30 it is weak and
decelerating on three points. The evaluation is at exactly (pi/2)^3; the
doubler at b = 0.30 may sit nearby.

Notes recorded, not gates:
  - The end-mode direction spread at |k| = 0.05 is 5.49e-4 against the paper's
    "about 5e-4" (about 10% high). The exponent is 2.0016 against 2.00.
  - Localisation length at |k| = 0.2 depends on direction: 0.603 to 0.653
    across the 13 directions. The paper does not state which direction its
    table uses.
  - The design's section 5 expectation that the Dirac-fit omega0 agrees with
    omega(k = 0) holds at N_s = 2 and 4 and fails at N_s = 8 and 12. The fit
    cannot resolve residual masses below about 2.5e-5. This is a resolution
    limit of the fit, not a finding about the physics.
