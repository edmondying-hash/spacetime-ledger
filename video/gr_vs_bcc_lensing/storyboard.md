## Data missing — what would permit a quantitative overlay later

A quantitative side-by-side (clocks, trajectory overlays, angle/time deltas) would require **all** of the following, with provenance — **none assumed present for this storyboard**:

1. **Matched scenario params:** source position, mass (or equivalent), impact parameter(s), domain size, units, boundary conditions — identical inputs declared for both models.
2. **Independent GR output:** null-geodesic / wavefront propagation from an established GR method (not hand-drawn), with documented code/data provenance.
3. **Independent BCC-derived propagation output:** from a fixed-node/link congestion/capacity model run on those same params — **not** a fitted or copied GR delay map, and not GR delays re-labeled as congestion.
4. **Comparable observables:** arrival times / deflection measures with stated units and uncertainty/limits.
5. **Clear labeling** that any residual mismatch or match is a model-comparison result, not empirical proof.

Without (1)–(4), keep overlays schematic and captions at hypothesis level (this draft).
